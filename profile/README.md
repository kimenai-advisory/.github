# Kimenai Advisory

**Cybersecurity and AI compliance advisory, from the Netherlands.**

We help organisations turn EU digital regulation into workable practice. That means combining legal analysis with hands-on security and AI governance.

## Focus areas

- **NIS2 / Cyberbeveiligingswet** – scoping, governance, risk management and incident-reporting readiness
- **EU AI Act** – AI system inventories, risk classification and governance frameworks
- **Cyber Resilience Act** – product security obligations for software and connected devices
- **Contracts and supply chain** – security and AI clauses in vendor and customer agreements

## How we work

- Regulation-first: every recommendation traces back to the legal obligation behind it
- Practical: deliverables your teams can actually implement
- Benelux and EU scope, in Dutch and English
